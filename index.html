ABOUT OUR COMPANY

NexaByte Solutions is a forward-thinking IT startup headquartered in India, specializing in custom software development, cloud consulting, and digital transformation for businesses of all sizes.

Founded in 2024, our team of passionate engineers, designers, and strategists has helped 50+ clients across fintech, healthcare, e-commerce, and logistics streamline their operations and unlock new revenue streams through technology.
